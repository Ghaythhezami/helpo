Helpo mockups

Every design version of Educator Helper, newest first. Each one is clickable; use the screen picker at the bottom of each mockup to move between screens. The PNG sets are the images shared on WhatsApp.

v6

Nibras, simplified (new)

Two teacher screens, no page scroll: Create (upload or describe, with Refine with AI, previous lessons underneath) and the Lesson workspace (5 tabs, edit in the margins, a draggable whiteboard with pencil and eraser, draw on slides). Students get one interface: join with a code and a name, then play the quiz or mini-game. English and Arabic, phone and laptop. The recommended version.
