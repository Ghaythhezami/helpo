Educator Helper v3, friendly for every age

Three kid-friendly, premium directions for grades 2 to 12, all in cool colours. The teacher screens are warm but still quick to use, and everything students see adapts to their age, from a 7-year-old's first quiz to a 17-year-old's revision.

One product, three age bands

Grades 2–5

Explorer

Big picture answers, read-aloud on every question, an early-reader font, one thing per screen and gentle "Not yet, try again".

Grades 6–8

Navigator

Friendly and lively without being babyish: a progress path, encouraging feedback and optional read-aloud.

Grades 9–12

Studio

Calm and mature like a good productivity app: no cartoons, keyboard shortcuts and precise feedback.

Recommendation

Build G. Sky Classroom as the product. It keeps the v2 flow teachers found fastest and makes it welcoming. Take two pieces from the others:

  • From H: the learning path as the student home, so every child sees their own progress without being compared to anyone.
  • From I: the chunky answer tiles for the Explorer band, because they are the easiest targets for 7-year-old fingers.
